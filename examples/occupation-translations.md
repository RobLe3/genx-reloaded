# Six fictional experience translations

Every fact below is invented for illustration and explicitly supplied as the candidate's evidence. These are editorial examples, not measured hiring interventions. Never copy an achievement into a real person's CV without their own evidence. The examples show action and scope when no business metric exists.

## 1. Manufacturing maintenance

**Target:** maintenance technician, fault diagnosis required, current PLC programming preferred.

**Before:** "Old factory maintenance work."

**Fact ledger:** diagnosed intermittent relay faults, 1995-2003; documented the checks in a maintenance log; no PLC programming; no downtime figures.

**After:** "Diagnosed intermittent relay faults and documented checks in the maintenance log, 1995-2003."

**Why:** preserves directly relevant diagnosis and its period. Current PLC work remains a gap. Neither modern programming nor a reduction in downtime is inferred. An interview can explore the diagnostic sequence without renaming the equipment.

## 2. Retail and stockroom work

**Target:** stockroom coordinator, stock accuracy and handovers required.

**Before:** "Shop work and warehouse duties."

**Fact ledger:** handled returns and trained two starters, 1998-2012; counted deliveries and recorded discrepancies on paper, 2013-2025; demonstrated a handover routine; never used ERP software.

**After:** "Counted deliveries and recorded discrepancies on paper, 2013-2025." "Handled customer returns and trained two new starters, 1998-2012."

**Why:** both periods provide useful evidence. Paper records do not become ERP experience. A small inventory practice exercise can be labelled as learning, separate from employment.

## 3. Hospitality

**Target:** restaurant shift coordinator.

**Before:** "Kept everything running smoothly."

**Fact ledger:** maintained the shift handover book, 2016-2024; briefed incoming kitchen colleagues about outstanding orders; no evidence about error rates or customer satisfaction.

**After:** "Maintained the shift handover book and briefed incoming kitchen colleagues on outstanding orders, 2016-2024."

**Why:** identifies an actual continuity practice. It does not claim fewer mistakes, formal line management, or safety certification. The contribution is meaningful without a percentage.

## 4. Community administration

**Target:** part-time school-office administrator.

**Before:** "Helped at the community centre."

**Fact ledger:** voluntary role, 2017-2023; coordinated room bookings; created a shared paper diary used by volunteers; no school employment or specialist education software experience.

**After:** "Volunteer booking coordinator, 2017-2023: coordinated community-centre room bookings and created a shared paper diary used by volunteers."

**Why:** makes scheduling evidence visible while retaining volunteer status. Do not convert it into paid school-office work or add a software skill from the target advertisement.

## 5. Caring and a career break

**Target:** care-service administrator, with scheduling experience preferred; not a registered nursing post.

**Before:** "Out of work."

**Fact ledger:** private family caring, 2020-2022; candidate wants neutral break wording; prior paid receptionist role, 2014-2019, included booking appointments; no nursing registration.

**After:** "Career break, 2020-2022." "Receptionist, 2014-2019: booked appointments and maintained the appointment diary."

**Why:** uses the relevant paid-work evidence without exposing the reason for the break. The caring history can support private confidence work, but cannot become nursing registration or unrequested public disclosure.

## 6. Creative production

**Target:** print-production coordinator.

**Before:** "Worked with old publishing systems."

**Fact ledger:** prepared print-ready catalogue files, 2002-2010; checked proofs with the printer; used period-specific desktop-publishing software; no current Adobe certification or digital-campaign metrics.

**After:** "Prepared print-ready catalogue files and checked proofs with the printer, 2002-2010."

**Why:** retains production handoff and proofing knowledge. Current software fluency needs its own evidence. A permission-cleared or fictional sample can demonstrate current practice without publishing a former client's files.

## Review any translation

Can each substantive statement be traced to the fact ledger? Does the original period remain clear? Is the contribution separate from a hoped-for outcome? Are a current credential or tool skill being inferred? Has private context been disclosed? If any answer is uncertain, narrow the wording or ask one focused question.

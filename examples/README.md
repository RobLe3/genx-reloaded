# Examples

All people, employers and candidate facts in these examples are fictional. Do not reuse their claims as a real person's history.

## Short before-and-after

| Supplied evidence | Useful wording | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Led six engineers through an 11-site network replacement in 2014 | "Led six engineers through a network replacement across 11 sites." Under the original role and period | Does not become a cloud migration or measured saving |
| Wrote 18 approved instructions and trained 24 operators; auditor certificate expired | "Authored 18 approved work instructions and trained 24 operators." | Certificate remains expired; training work remains relevant |
| Completed a Power BI introduction and a personal exercise | "Power BI: introductory course and personal practice." | Does not become production delivery |
| Candidate privately disclosed medical reasons for a break | "Career break, 2020-2022", if these dates and wording are confirmed | Private explanation stays out unless the candidate chooses disclosure |

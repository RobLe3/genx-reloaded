# Australia

Checked 12 October 2026. Recognise AU and AUS. Separate Australian Public Service applications from state/territory public sectors and commercial employers.

## Resume, pitch and selection criteria

Use the vacancy's requested resume/CV format and spelling. Start with readable, relevant evidence and a clear chronology; do not impose a US one-page rule on a long career. Default to leaving photos, birth date, marital status, tax and identity numbers out of ordinary application copy. Supply required eligibility information only through the specified process.

[APSC's application guidance](https://www.apsc.gov.au/working-aps/joining-aps/cracking-code/3-applying-aps-job-cracking-code) explains that a CV may need a separate pitch, selection-criteria response, or answers to questions. Check the information pack and word/page limits. Do not replace a requested pitch with a generic cover letter or assume every APS role wants the same structure. Use one or two strong candidate-supported examples to explain personal actions and results. A commercial or community example can establish a capability without claiming prior APS employment or a classification the candidate has not held.

Keep older evidence if it is the best fit. For overseas trades and professions, retain original awards and distinguish qualification assessment, occupational registration/licensing and migration processes. Verify the exact regulator and state/territory when these matter; do not add Australian registration from experience alone.

## Referees, privacy and rights

Follow requested referee fields, checking each person's agreement and the candidate's contact timing. Do not nominate a current manager automatically. Offer a short request to defer contact if confidentiality matters.

[AHRC age-discrimination guidance](https://www.humanrights.gov.au/our-work/complaint-information-service/complaints-under-age-discrimination-act) was available in search extracts; full-page retrieval failed in this review. Treat it as a starting point and retrieve current official guidance before a procedural/legal answer. Do not borrow US age thresholds, UK procedures, or automatic AI opt-out claims. Ask for only the jurisdiction and facts needed for the next useful step.

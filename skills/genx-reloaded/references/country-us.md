# United States

Checked 12 October 2026. Use for the job's US market, including US/USA. These are scoped starting points, not a complete federal/state/local legal guide.

## Choose the application type

For a commercial role, draft a focused resume with relevant experience, plain headings and supported results. Treat roughly two readable pages as an editorial starting point for a substantial career, not a legal limit or universal ATS rule. For academic/research roles, inspect the requested CV, publications and other materials separately. Default to omitting photographs, birth date, marital status and government identifiers from ordinary application copy; handle necessary eligibility information through the specified process.

For federal jobs, inspect the current announcement and [OPM applicant guidance](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/hiring-information/merit-hiring-plan-resources/applicant-guidance-on-the-two-page-resume-limit/). The guidance implements a **two-page resume limit from 27 September 2025** for USAJOBS applications. Do not recommend a historical four-to-six-page federal resume. Relevant entries need employer/title, month/year dates, hours per week, and federal series/grade where applicable. Keep qualifying duties and required evidence within the limit; gather missing facts rather than guess hours or months. Check separate supporting-document requirements and current announcement-specific instructions, including any specialised CV route.

## Translate experience without changing identity

Use US spelling where requested. Explain unfamiliar overseas titles alongside originals; do not invent US degrees, licences, clearance, veteran eligibility or work authorisation. Preserve strong older evidence with its period. A concise resume and a complete requested application history can coexist.

## Rights and next steps

[EEOC age guidance](https://www.eeoc.gov/age-discrimination) describes federal ADEA protection for people aged 40 and above and notes that state laws can differ. Verify employer coverage, location, current exceptions and deadlines for a concrete concern. Do not infer an unlawful rejection or a universal AI opt-out from age, timing, or vendor branding. Offer a factual process/accommodation question when useful; the candidate chooses disclosure.

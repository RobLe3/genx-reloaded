# Ashby

Checked 12 October 2026. Scope: AI-Assisted Application Review, not all Ashby features.

**Documented:** [AI-Assisted Application Review](https://docs.ashbyhq.com/ai-assisted-application-review) requires enablement and employer-defined criteria. Evaluations can be meets, does not meet, or undecided, and support filtering and a percentage-of-criteria view. This is distinct from the human reviewers' Average Score. The page describes configurable automated-processing notices and opt-out handling. Application origin can affect whether the notice is seen and evaluations are shown.

**Help the candidate:** Make relevant evidence and its context explicit, without guessing hidden criteria or adding unsupported keywords. If concerned about AI review, inspect the actual job's notice and available option. Ask the employer about its process when unclear. Keep source facts distinct from assumptions about deployment, thresholds or the reason for rejection.

**Avoid:** Promising every Ashby application uses this feature or offers the same control; interpreting another site's 'ATS score' as Ashby's score; asserting that an undecided evaluation is a rejection. Do not turn differing notice behaviour across referrals and hosted forms into a bypass strategy. A privacy choice is not an established route to higher hiring odds.

The source is vendor-authored and does not independently establish fairness or candidate outcomes. Recheck feature documentation and notices before consequential advice.

# United Kingdom

Checked 12 October 2026. Distinguish England, Scotland and Wales from Northern Ireland when legal routes matter. Do not assume a UK-wide rights procedure.

## CV and application adaptation

[National Careers Service guidance](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/cv-sections), an England service, recommends clear headings, recent-first work history and omitting age, birth date, marital status and nationality. It also advises against putting referees' contact details on the CV. Apply these as practical starting points, not legislation or a claim that every UK employer uses identical conventions.

Use UK spelling and preserve original qualifications, with a factual explanation when useful. Do not convert an overseas award into GCSEs, A levels or a UK degree without verified recognition. Keep a strong older example with its dates. Use a short, neutral career-break label if wanted; do not insert private caring or health reasons.

For public-service roles, read the application pack before shortening material. [Civil Service Success Profiles](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/success-profiles) separates behaviours, strengths, ability, experience and technical evidence, with different elements used for different roles. Draft the requested personal statement or behaviour examples as well as the CV, within the stated limits. Follow any name-blind instructions on the actual form. Do not apply this framework automatically to the NHS, local authorities, or the Northern Ireland Civil Service. An NHS supporting-information field needs evidence against that vacancy's person specification, not merely a duplicate CV.

## Rights and privacy

Use [GOV.UK's discrimination starting point](https://www.gov.uk/discrimination-your-rights) and [Northern Ireland's age guidance](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/age-discrimination) for the relevant jurisdiction, then verify the specific route before giving procedural advice. Separate voluntary monitoring forms from CV content. Required right-to-work checks do not justify inventing nationality or adding sensitive documents to a public CV. Keep complaints and adjustments factual and candidate-controlled.

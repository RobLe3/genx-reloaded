# Tellent Recruitee

Checked 12 October 2026. Scope: documented CV extraction and email-inbox processing.

**Documented:** [Why was my candidate's CV not parsed correctly?](https://support.recruitee.com/en/articles/4752121-why-was-my-candidate-s-cv-not-parsed-correctly), dated 30 May 2024, describes third-party parsing. For CVs sent to the company/job inbox, a missing email address can prevent recognition/parsing. A single file combining CV, cover letter and references can cause another person's email to be selected. Visual, scanned, protected or awkwardly structured documents can also impair extraction.

**Help the candidate:** Check that their intended contact email is readable in the CV body. Keep referee details and supporting documents separate when the form permits. If the employer explicitly requests a combined dossier, follow that requirement and make the candidate's identity/contact clear at the start; ask for correction if another person's data appears.

**If blocked:** Supply a corrected file through an allowed route and ask the employer to correct the record. Recruiter-side conversion of an inbox item into a profile is not a candidate control.

**Avoid:** Generalising the inbox-specific missing-email rule to every application route, claiming email avoids ATS processing, or replacing a real referee address with invented data. The documented parser limitations do not establish rejection rules or a hiring score.

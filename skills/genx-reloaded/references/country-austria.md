# Austria: application choices

Checked 12 October 2026. Use for a job in Austria. Language alone does not determine jurisdiction.

## Verified starting point

The [Gleichbehandlungsanwaltschaft's job-search guidance](https://www.gleichbehandlungsanwaltschaft.gv.at/Themen/Diskriminierung-bei-der-Jobsuche.html) describes restrictions on discriminatory job advertisements and offers free advice. It includes exceptions for characteristics that are essential to the work. A phrase in an advertisement or a rejection alone does not settle the legal assessment.

## Apply to the candidate's task

Read the employer's requested documents, language and selection questions. Preserve original titles, apprenticeships, qualification names, dates and stated language levels. Explain their meaning without claiming formal equivalence. Clarify employer acceptance separately from regulated-profession recognition.

Ask only what materially changes the draft. Do not add a photograph, date of birth, nationality or private career-break explanation by default. Treat this as a privacy-conscious editorial choice, not a statement that every field is prohibited by Austrian law. If the candidate wants a different presentation, accommodate it honestly.

Keep compensation information in the advertisement separate from the candidate's target and any verified legal interpretation. Do not infer that age or a career change requires a pay reduction.

A neutral German clarification, when relevant: "Akzeptieren Sie für diese Anforderung auch einschlägige Berufserfahrung?" Do not invent experience that meets the condition or send the question automatically.

## Check before going further

Verify current official rules for the specific employment context, qualification recognition, work authorization, photographs, mandatory form fields, remedies and deadlines. This card does not establish a universal Austrian CV format or transfer German AGG procedures to Austria. Use the official advice channel when the candidate wants help assessing a concrete concern.

## 1. Meaning and history of the term

Mary Ellen Slayter, SmartBrief, **Do you need to “Botox” your resume?**, page dated 26 May 2016.

https://www.smartbrief.com/original/do-you-need-botox-your-resume

The interview with Lisa Johnson Mandell uses the metaphor for moving strong current qualifications forward and removing age cues such as graduation dates. It establishes usage by 2016, not the term's first appearance. The reported job-search success is anecdotal and bundled with other changes, so it cannot identify a causal effect of CV edits. The label is not a formal recruitment method or a women-only technique. Full page inspected.

## 2. Age and gender in hiring

David Neumark, Ian Burn, Patrick Button, Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco, **Age Discrimination and Hiring of Older Workers**, 27 February 2017, Economic Letter 2017-06.

https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/el2017-06.pdf

The authors summarize their field experiment with more than 40,000 applications to US administrative, retail, security, and janitorial jobs. Older women received fewer callbacks; for administrative roles, women aged 64 to 66 had a 7.6% callback rate versus 14.4% for ages 29 to 31, a relative decline of about 47%. Male results were less consistent. This supports concern about age bias in the occupations studied. It does not test removing graduation dates, identify AI as the mechanism, or estimate the effect for senior AI architects in Germany. Full report inspected.

## 3. Direct evidence that writing quality can matter

Emma van Inwegen, Zanele Munyikwa, John J. Horton, **Algorithmic Writing Assistance on Jobseekers' Resumes Increases Hires**, arXiv version 1, 19 January 2023.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08083

https://arxiv.org/html/2301.08083v1

This research paper reports a randomized experiment involving nearly half a million new online-platform jobseekers. Writing assistance increased hiring probability by approximately 8% relative, not eight percentage points; the control rate was about 3%. The intervention addressed writing errors and clarity. It did not test age concealment, generative rewriting, or this skill. The researchers found no evidence of reduced employer satisfaction. Use it as bounded causal support for clear writing, not a promised uplift for a particular applicant. Version 1 text inspected; do not silently substitute another version's estimates.

AARP, **How to Write a Resume After 50**.

https://www.aarp.org/work/job-search/resume-writing-after-50/

Accessible search excerpts recommend focusing on relevant responsibilities from the preceding 15 years. This is practitioner guidance, not a controlled result or mandatory rule. ## 5. Actual parser limitations

Greenhouse Support, **Unsuccessful resume parse**, updated 2 March 2026.

https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse

Official documentation identifies image resumes, columns, text boxes, and complex formatting as possible causes of partial or failed extraction. It says failed parsing can require manual data entry; it does not establish automatic rejection. This supports checking the final file's text and simplifying problematic layouts. It does not imply identical behavior across vendors. Full page inspected. Verify current documentation before citing file limits or other product-specific constraints.

## 6. Interpreting results and uncertainty

No controlled evaluation of the complete package called CV Botoxing was identified in this bounded search. That is a search finding, not proof that none exists. Age discrimination evidence, writing-quality experiments, parser documentation, and career-coaching advice answer different questions and must remain distinct.

Apply these editorial judgments transparently:

- Lead with supported present capability and use chronology to substantiate it.
- Reduce irrelevant history while preserving the candidate's strongest evidence.
- Let the candidate decide whether to omit optional personal information.
- Distinguish demonstrated competence from unverified claims and missing evidence.
- Do not assume that a shorter CV, omitted date, photo-free layout, or new keyword necessarily improves interview chances.
- Do not explain an individual rejection through discrimination or AI without evidence of that mechanism.

The skill's audit categories and workflow are practical synthesis. They have not been psychometrically validated or tested in a hiring field experiment. Passing local document checks or a synthetic skill exercise cannot establish effectiveness in recruitment.

## 7. Automated age exclusion: an enforcement case

US Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, **iTutorGroup to Pay $365,000 to Settle EEOC Discriminatory Hiring Suit**, 11 September 2023.

https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/itutorgroup-pay-365000-settle-eeoc-discriminatory-hiring-suit

The EEOC reported a settlement of claims that recruiting software automatically rejected older applicants. The decree provided $365,000 and non-monetary relief. Describe the discrimination claims as allegations resolved by settlement, not a trial judgment or proof about all hiring software. This is a concrete reason to take automated age exclusion seriously; it does not establish that other generations fear Gen X. Agency release inspected.

## 12. Indirect age cues and mixed anonymisation effects

**Implicit Age Cues in Resumes: Subtle Effects on Hiring Discrimination**, Frontiers in Psychology, 2017, DOI 10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01321.

https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01321/full

An experimental study of HR ratings found that indirect cues affected age inferences even when explicit age information was absent. Omitting explicit age cues did not uniformly improve suitability ratings in that study. No evidence of in-group favoritism by recruiter age was found. These are controlled rating results, not deployed-ATS behaviour or a rule for every market. They caution against blanket promises about date removal and generational motives. Findings and discussion inspected.

https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/hes-create-impactful-resumes-and-cover-letters/

Career guidance emphasises specific, accurate, concise, tailored writing and results. This is advice, not a controlled hiring study.

Bundesagentur fuer Arbeit, **Bewerbungsunterlagen: Das gehoert rein**, undated page inspected 12 October 2026.

https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/bildung/bewerbung/bewerbungsunterlagen

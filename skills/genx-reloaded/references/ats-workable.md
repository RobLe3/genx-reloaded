# Workable

Checked 12 October 2026. Distinguish direct application, job-board origin and employer review state.

**Documented:** [Candidate import guidance](https://jobseekers.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036231313-Import-your-resume-details-to-autofill-the-application), updated 7 October 2026, specifies up to 5 MB and PDF, DOC, DOCX, ODT or RTF for this import route. [Auto-disqualification](https://help.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/115012238688-Auto-disqualify-candidates-using-application-form-questions) can use configured 'No' answers to yes/no questions; affected candidates still receive an application confirmation.

[Old-file guidance](https://jobseekers.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/20029890356887-Why-is-my-old-resume-cover-letter-being-added-to-applications), updated 30 September 2026, identifies LinkedIn Easy Apply saved resumes/cover letters as one origin. [Updating an application](https://jobseekers.workable.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036233113-Updating-an-application), updated 11 October 2026, describes reapplying before employer review, or withdrawing and applying again, but warns withdrawal cancels existing events, video interviews and assessment invitations.

**Help the candidate:** Check the originating surface's selected file, then the form and attachment. For a submitted-file correction with an active interview, draft a request to the employer to update it while preserving the invitation. Do not withdraw or resubmit automatically. Check current guidance and deadlines before any candidate-chosen correction.

**Avoid:** Treating receipt as proof of passing screening, applying the 5 MB import limit to every Workable upload, or assuming a LinkedIn fix applies to another job board. Truthful eligibility answers remain essential.

# Teamtailor

Checked 12 October 2026. Scope: Mailbox email ingestion, not every candidate feature.

**Documented:** [Transfer email applications into your recruitment process](https://support.teamtailor.com/en/articles/9208152-transfer-email-applications-into-your-recruitment-process-in-teamtailor), dated 2 September 2026, describes a job-specific mailbox that creates a candidate card and attempts to parse attached files into basic contact information.

**Help the candidate:** If the employer offers an email route, keep the intended resume readable, its identity/contact information clear, and the vacancy reference accurate. Use only the address supplied through a verified employer channel. Keep a sent copy and any confirmation if wanted. If data is wrong, draft a correction request referring to that job.

**Avoid:** Recommending email as a guaranteed ATS bypass or guessing a mailbox address from the job number. A personal email to a recruiter may also enter a system, but do not claim this specific Mailbox feature processed it without evidence. No candidate-visible scoring, universal attachment limit or automatic rejection rule was verified by this source.

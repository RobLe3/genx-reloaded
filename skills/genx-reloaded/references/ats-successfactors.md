# SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting

Checked 12 October 2026. Scope: public SAP knowledge-base documentation.

**Documented:** [KBA 2733185](https://userapps.support.sap.com/sap/support/knowledge/en/2733185) explains that resume parsing fills standard fields rather than custom fields; a field configured as custom may therefore stay empty. The article also notes a date-field limitation, without establishing a resolution date here.

**Help the candidate:** When a resume attaches but employment or qualification fields remain blank, inspect the actual fields and fill editable ones faithfully. Compare dates with the source, especially after an overwrite/import. Do not spend the candidate's energy repeatedly redesigning a readable CV to fix a configuration limitation.

**If blocked:** Capture the field label and redacted error; ask the employer to check the candidate-profile configuration or provide a correction route. Do not give the candidate recruiter/admin settings as though they can change them.

**Avoid:** Claiming every empty field proves a poor CV, that this KBA certifies current behaviour in every release, or that SuccessFactors and SAP's other recruiting products share identical parsing rules. Recheck the current KBA and employer form before giving specific bug advice. Ranking, automatic rejection and exact file limits were not established by this source.

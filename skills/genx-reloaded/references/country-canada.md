# Canada

Checked 12 October 2026. CA means Canada when the context establishes it; do not silently treat a California location as Canadian.

## Resume and language

[Job Bank's resume guidance](https://www.nl.jobbank.gc.ca/findajob/resources/write-good-resume) advises a concise targeted resume, no photograph or Social Insurance Number, and references supplied separately when requested. It recommends two pages and minimising older experience. Treat page length as guidance, not an ATS rule, and retain genuinely relevant older evidence with its period. Do not adopt its unsupported universal skim-time claim or turn its 15-year suggestion into mandatory deletion.

Check the vacancy's language. For Quebec and bilingual roles, offer the requested French and/or English version, keeping names, credentials, dates and proficiency consistent. Do not infer bilingual ability or a legal language requirement from location alone. Verify a specific language-law question with the relevant authority. Give overseas employers and duties enough context to be understood; overseas experience does not become Canadian experience by rewriting it.

## Credentials and jurisdiction

[Federal credential-recognition guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/foreign-credential-recognition.html) directs regulated-profession and compulsory-trade applicants to the regulator in the intended province or territory. Preserve the original award and any actual assessment status. Do not equate an immigration assessment, translation or long experience with permission to practise or use a protected title.

[CHRC jurisdiction guidance](https://www.chrc-ccdp.gc.ca/find-help/file-discrimination-complaint/find-out-if-you-are-right-place) separates federal complaints from provincial/territorial routes. Ask about the employer and province only when it changes a concrete rights answer. Do not apply US ADEA rules or presume every national company is federally regulated.

For public-service applications, inspect the actual screening questions, language profile and eligibility conditions. Provide examples showing where, when and how the candidate meets each requested qualification. Do not answer only 'see resume', invent public-service levels, or imply work authorisation.

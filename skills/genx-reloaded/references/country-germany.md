# Germany: application choices

Checked 12 October 2026. Use for a job in Germany, not automatically for a German-speaking person elsewhere. This is a limited country card, not a complete legal guide.

## Verified starting points

- [Bundesagentur für Arbeit](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/bildung/bewerbung/bewerbungsunterlagen) describes the application photo as optional while noting customary use. Its page targets younger applicants; do not import its school-document checklist wholesale into a senior application.
- [AGG section 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/agg/__1.html) names age among its protected grounds. This alone does not establish a breach, remedy, deadline, or the outcome of a complaint. Verify the applicable provisions and circumstances when a legal question arises.

## Apply to the candidate's task

Follow the actual advertisement's language and requested documents. Preserve qualification names, such as "Fachinformatiker Systemintegration", with a factual explanation when needed. Do not translate vocational training into a bachelor's degree. Keep B2 as B2.

Offer photo and optional personal-detail choices without asserting that omission guarantees better treatment. Preserve truthful chronology. A form requesting complete history is a different task from selecting achievements for a short CV.

If asked to explain mixed-language documents, put the explanation inside the requested letter. Example when accurate: "Meinen Lebenslauf reiche ich auf Englisch ein; dieses Anschreiben habe ich auf Deutsch verfasst." Add an international-work rationale only if the candidate supplied it.

Use "Berufliche Auszeit, 2020-2022" only when those facts are supplied and neutral wording is wanted. Do not add medical or family context from private conversation.

## Check before going further

Verify profession-specific recognition, work authorization, mandatory disclosures, access rights, complaint routes and deadlines from current official sources. The anti-discrimination agency website was not retrievable during this release's research; no uninspected procedural advice is supplied here. Do not guess a deadline or import Austrian or Swiss rules.

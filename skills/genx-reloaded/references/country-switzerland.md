# Switzerland: application choices

Checked 12 October 2026. Use for a job in Switzerland. Confirm the requested working language; do not infer it from the country alone.

## Verified starting point

[FDPIC guidance on employment phases](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/de/verschiedene-phasen-des-arbeitsverhaeltnisses) addresses employment under private law. It limits applicant information to what is relevant to suitability and says employers need the applicant's prior consent before seeking information from third parties. Its scope and exceptions matter. Do not apply it unchanged to every public-sector process or make universal claims about retention periods.

## Apply to the candidate's task

Follow the vacancy's language and document instructions. Preserve the actual qualification and its country of award. Do not equate an overseas qualification with a Swiss credential without verified recognition. Keep employment periods and credential validity accurate.

Separate a request for references from permission to contact a named person. Draft a response for the candidate to approve; never contact a current employer merely because their name appears in a CV. Do not include a referee's private contact details in a public example.

Offer neutral break wording and minimal optional personal details. These are editorial options, not an assertion that all Swiss employers require or reject a particular photo, birth date or layout.

Example when accurate: "Referenzen stelle ich nach Rücksprache gerne zur Verfügung." Use only if the candidate is willing to do so.

## Check before going further

Verify work authorization, regulated-profession recognition, public/private employment scope, local procedures, and any requested legal remedy. Do not import EU or UK automated-decision rights into Switzerland. Recheck official guidance before advising on a concrete disclosure or data request.

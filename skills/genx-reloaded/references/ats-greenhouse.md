# Greenhouse Recruiting

Checked 12 October 2026. Vendor documentation, no tenant test.

**Documented:** [Unsuccessful resume parse](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse), updated 2 March 2026, describes failed/partial field extraction, including complex layouts and resumes larger than 2.5 MB. A file can remain attached despite extraction failure. This is a parsing limit, not a universal upload limit or rejection decision.

[Talent Matching FAQ](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/41131886674075-Talent-Matching-FAQ), updated 5 October 2026, describes configured criteria and match categories. This feature does not itself advance/reject candidates. Its AI opt-out is employer-enabled and feature-specific. Separately, [Auto-reject](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360000653472-Auto-reject) documents rules on selected application-question answers.

**Help the candidate:** For extraction trouble, inspect text and correct visible fields, keeping real employer/title information. For a quick rejection, inspect the actual questions before rewriting keywords. For AI concerns, inspect the actual notice and offered control. Explain that employer settings and the cause of a rejection remain unknown unless evidenced.

**Avoid:** Promising that Greenhouse never auto-rejects, that low matching automatically rejects, or that everyone has the same opt-out. Do not add fictitious company suffixes or modern titles merely because the parser documentation mentions them. Recruiter-side repair controls may require asking the employer for help.

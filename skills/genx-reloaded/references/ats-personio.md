# Personio Recruiting

Checked 12 October 2026. Scope: [CV parsing for candidate profiles](https://support.personio.de/hc/en-us/articles/360010193018-CV-parsing-for-candidate-profiles), updated 14 July 2026.

**Documented:** Personio uses Textkernel for CV parsing when the employer enables it. The page describes extraction of contact/current-role attributes and years of experience, and warns about graphics, scans, headers/footers and complex layouts. It also says gender may be derived from other resume details. The subcontractor enable/disable setting belongs to the employer, not the applicant.

**Help the candidate:** Keep contact information and chronology in readable text, retain original dates and make separate roles clear. If a derived attribute is wrong or concerning, draft a factual correction/process question to the employer or its privacy contact. Explain that a documented inference capability does not prove this employer used it or used it in selection.

**Avoid:** Advising a name change or gender disguise, presenting employer subcontractor settings as a candidate opt-out, or claiming omission of birth date removes all age proxies. Preserve the person's identity and choose optional disclosures deliberately. Do not generalise the manual recruiter-import file limits to all candidate forms.

No live extraction accuracy, legal compliance determination or independent bias evaluation was performed. Verify the current notice, local rights and actual process before advising a rights request.

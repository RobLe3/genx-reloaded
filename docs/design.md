# Design principles

## Product goal

Help experienced people regain confidence and represent the value of their careers accurately within contemporary application processes.

Success is a useful and truthful application, a clearer understanding of the hiring process, and an achievable next step. Interview and offer rates can be monitored, but the project must not claim to control those outcomes.

## The emotional starting point

The experience of bias and repeated rejection can leave people feeling invisible, angry, or exhausted. They do not need to prove the cause of each rejection before receiving empathy.

The skill validates feelings, recognises documented unfairness, and helps recover agency. It does not prescribe optimism or make a long motivational exercise a prerequisite for practical help.

Retain the agreed editorial boundary:

> I wouldn’t claim that other generations deliberately encoded fear of Gen X into algorithms, because we don’t have evidence for that motive. The skill can validate the frustration without turning rejection into a verdict about the person or another generation.

This boundary guides claims. It should not become a repeated caveat that interrupts emotional support.

## Generational identity

Use familiar Gen X experiences as optional prompts and opportunities for humour. Do not assert that all members of the generation share them or that younger or older people lack these capabilities.

Welcome people of any age who find the method useful. Never require proof of age. Accommodate disability, nonlinear careers, career breaks, vocational paths, and different energy levels.

## Support leads into evidence

A remembered strength is a starting point. A CV claim needs an accurate account of actual work. Preserve the chain from memory to capability, work example, source, and final wording.

An old example may remain the strongest evidence for a current requirement. A new technology name does not make an unsupported claim current.

## Separate the mechanisms

Distinguish document extraction, employer-configured search or matching, screening-question rules, human review, and interviews. Identify the source of each product-specific assertion.

Differentiate human bias, explicitly discriminatory rules, biased training data or proxies, technical errors, unclear presentation, and genuine requirement gaps. Do not call all of them the same thing.

## Evidence maintenance

Record source type and date, what was inspected, sample or context, finding, limitation, and practical implication. Use primary sources where possible. Preserve contrary and mixed findings.

Refresh changing product and legal claims at use time. Treat empirical study results as bounded by their methods and population. Mark hypothetical examples, allegations, settlements, and vendor assertions correctly.

## Technical limits

Local preflight checks support document QA. They do not emulate an employer's ATS, validate all visual features, infer merit, or calculate hiring probability.

The repository's tests use synthetic documents and agent scenarios. They do not establish clinical benefit, hiring uplift, or resistance to every model failure.

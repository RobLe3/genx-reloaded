# Bias evidence map

Use the [runtime evidence register](../skills/genx-reloaded/references/evidence.md) for primary links and the [claim register](claims.json) for the refreshed ICO records. This is a scoped map, not exhaustive coverage of selection bias.

| Evidence | What it addresses | What it does not settle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neumark, Burn and Button field experiment, legacy section 2 | Age and gender differences in callbacks within studied US occupations | Senior roles in DACH, a particular rejection, or the effect of deleting dates |
| Indirect age-cue experiment, legacy section 12 | Age inference and suitability ratings under controlled conditions | Live ATS behaviour; results do not support universal anonymisation benefits |
| EEOC iTutorGroup settlement, legacy section 7 | An enforcement allegation about software age exclusion resolved by settlement | A trial finding or prevalence across vendors |
| ICO 2024 audits, GX-C01 | Provider practices and observed governance concerns | A representative prevalence estimate |
| ICO 2026 engagement, GX-C02 | Employer automation and review practices | An audit, causal experiment or diagnosis of individual discrimination |

Open research includes disability and access barriers, perceived ethnicity and names, migration and qualification recognition, class signals, veteran transitions, caring gaps, and intersections with gender and age. These are questions to investigate, not claims that the current register has established every mechanism. Distinguish field outcomes from model benchmarks and perceived treatment.

For assistant-side fairness checks, compare equally evidenced fictional careers with different irrelevant identity cues. Assess helpfulness, factual fidelity and agency using the same rubric. Do not infer demographics or rank candidates. A paired scenario evaluates assistant behaviour only. Report differences as observations in those executions, with sample limitations.

Evidence of a disadvantage does not prove a proposed remedy works. Date removal, title explanation, concise writing and human contact need separate intervention evidence. Keep mixed findings and distinguish a reasonable candidate option from a demonstrated hiring benefit.

# Candidate discovery and usability kit

**Prepared, not launched.** No participants have been recruited, interviewed or recorded for this release. Before inviting anyone, a study owner must confirm a private storage location, access list, contact route, actual retention period, withdrawal process, compensation arrangements and applicable ethics/privacy requirements. Do not publish a consent form with these operational decisions unresolved.

## Invitation draft for later approval

We are developing a CV and career-support skill and want to learn where it helps or gets in the way. You may use a fictional example, skip any question, take a break or stop. You do not need to provide your age, health history, full CV or employer names. This is product research, not recruitment or a promise of employment. We will explain data handling and any compensation before you decide whether to participate.

This text is a draft only. Do not send it or recruit people without authorization.

## Consent choices to record separately

1. Participation in the described session.
2. Optional use of the participant's own document instead of a fictional task.
3. Optional audio/screen recording, with an equally valid notes-only route.
4. Optional publication of a specifically approved quotation, separate from participation.
5. Optional follow-up contact, with a specified purpose and duration.

Explain what can be withdrawn, how to request it, and any limit after aggregated publication before consent. Do not promise deletion from every provider without checking the actual environment. No secondary training use is included. Keep consent and contact information separate from coded observations. Exclude raw records and identifiable quotations from Git.

## Exploratory interview, about 20 to 30 minutes if comfortable

- What were you trying to accomplish the last time you worked on an application?
- Where did it become difficult or tiring?
- What help would have saved effort?
- What advice or encouragement felt unhelpful?
- Does the GenX Reloaded name fit you? What does "CV Botoxing" suggest to you?
- What would make you trust or distrust a rewritten paragraph?
- What would you want a future session to remember, and what should it leave out?

Ask for an example without requiring private details. Do not lead the participant toward agreement about bias, age or the brand. If they decline a topic, move on. Do not turn the session into therapy or ask them to relive a distressing rejection.

## Usability tasks

Use equivalent fictional facts when comparing variants. Let the participant choose an accessible response format.

1. Start without a CV using one short work example.
2. Turn an older unmeasured contribution into a relevant bullet.
3. Correct a model suggestion that overstates a qualification.
4. Request immediate practical help with no encouragement.
5. Pause and resume from a minimal note, leaving private context out.
6. Review an application with a preferred skill gap and a misleading job-fee request.

Observe time, corrections, help requested, skipped steps and abandonment. Do not count a pause as failure. Ask briefly: did you feel understood, in control, pressured, or misrepresented? What would you change? Acceptance alone does not establish factual accuracy.

## Sampling and analysis

Begin with roughly 8 to 12 discovery interviews and iterative rounds of about 5 to 8 usability participants if resources permit. These ranges support exploration, not representativeness or subgroup inference. Seek occupational, language and access diversity without requiring sensitive disclosures. Record recruitment channel and who is missing.

Use coded observation rows: task, observation, participant's optional explanation, severity, design hypothesis, contrary example, proposed change. Separate observation from interpretation. Publish aggregate themes and synthetic reproductions only. Track disagreements and no-change findings. Record any researcher assistance that affected task completion.

The completion gate is a documented set of actual observations supporting prioritised changes, not merely a completed script. Until then, mark human usability and emotional effects unestablished.

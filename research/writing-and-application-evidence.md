# Writing quality and application completeness

Checked 12 October 2026. This note separates reasons to improve an application from evidence that GenX Reloaded itself improves hiring outcomes.

## A relevant field experiment

Wiles, Munyikwa and Horton, [Algorithmic Writing Assistance on Jobseekers' Resumes Increases Hires](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.04528), Management Science 71(12), 10144-10164, published online 18 April 2025.

The published abstract reports a randomised field experiment involving nearly half a million jobseekers on an online labour market. Nongenerative writing assistance was associated with 8% more hires and 10% higher wages in the treated group. The authors report clearer writing with fewer errors and no evidence of lower employer satisfaction.

Only the publisher's abstract and publication metadata were inspected here. Full methods, uncertainty estimates and subgroup results remain unreviewed. This was a different intervention and setting. These percentages are not estimates for Gen X, this skill, generative CV rewriting or ATS acceptance. Use the result as motivation for clear writing, not a marketing promise.

## The mechanism depends on the application

Greenhouse's [Talent Matching guide](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/41396009937307-Talent-Matching), updated 5 October 2026, describes employer-selected, weighted criteria and highlights related resume text. It is an optional assistive feature; the guide says it does not itself advance or reject applicants. Its separate [auto-reject rules](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360000653472-Auto-reject) can act on configured application-question answers. These are different mechanisms.

The editorial inference is to make supported evidence visible in the actual assessed fields and answer screening questions accurately. It does not follow that keywords alone determine a score, that every employer enables these features, or that a more readable document overcomes a missing mandatory qualification.

## What would establish a useful gain

The [prospective application comparison](../docs/application-gains.md) tests intermediate outcomes: factual evidence, field completeness, bounded experience calculations, usable files and correction work. It does not run an employer's parser or observe hiring.

Keep the stages separate when collecting future evidence:

| Stage | An observable outcome | What it cannot establish alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local file | Valid file, readable render and accurate extraction | Employer parser success |
| Authorised test environment | Correct field extraction, required answers and recorded submission state | Shortlisting or lack of bias |
| Human review | Relevant evidence found and understood, with measured correction effort | Hiring probability |
| Consented outcome study | Interview or offer outcomes under a suitable comparison design | Universal ATS compatibility |

Use permitted testing environments and fictional records for technical checks. Do not send duplicate real applications to manufacture an A/B test. Candidate experience, vacancy fit, market, application channel and employer configuration remain important differences to record. The prepared [candidate study kit](candidate-study-kit.md) is a starting point for the separate human evaluation.

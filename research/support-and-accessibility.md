# Support and accessibility: adaptation brief

## Decision

Retain optional encouragement and add explicit one-question, skip, stop, no-table, and private handover routes. These are design choices to evaluate with people, not a validated coaching treatment.

## Basis and limits

GX-C06 records the job-search intervention synthesis. Its aggregate and component findings motivate studying practical help together with support. An abstract does not provide enough information to reproduce the programmes or identify the effect of individual phrases. GX-C07 records a JOBS II original-study lead whose full methods were not retrieved. We do not adopt a clinical protocol or screening instrument.

GX-C08 records W3C guidance on orientation after interruption. We adapt the principle to a short resume note rather than require a dashboard. The candidate's ability to pause matters more than making them complete an intake sequence. Cognitive accessibility guidance does not establish that this host works with a specific screen reader or voice-input system.

## Hypotheses for user research

| Hypothesis | Task | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief acknowledgement followed by action feels respectful | Start after repeated rejection | Perceived respect, pressure, usefulness and preference for directness |
| One question reduces initial effort | Begin without a CV | Completion, clarification burden and whether the first question fits |
| A minimal resume note reduces repetition | Resume after an interruption | Facts recovered, corrections, private details unnecessarily repeated |
| Choice of format improves access | Same task with voice input or no tables | Observed blockers and participant preference, not a diagnosis |

Offer neutral, warm and gently defiant phrasing as candidate preferences. Do not assume the brand's voice works for everyone. Rejecting encouragement is a valid preference, not resistance to be overcome. Evaluate whether "Botoxing" feels gendered or implies hiding age; use GenX Reloaded as the default name meanwhile.

## Implemented and still open

Implemented: concise runtime routes, a handover pattern, prompt-level scenarios and a consented-study script. Open: actual participant observations, assistive-technology checks, comparative voice preference, full intervention-method review, and any causal outcome claim. Synthetic dialogue can expose obvious errors but cannot substitute for these studies.

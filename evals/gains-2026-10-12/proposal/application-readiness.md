# Prepare the complete application

Use when a vacancy and career evidence are available, especially with separate form answers, strict limits or a requested upload. Apply this as a working method; do not make the candidate read the checklist. Respect audit-only, stop and short-copy requests.

## 1. Fix the application requirements before rewriting

Extract the requested outputs, accepted file type/size, page or word limits, named fields, anonymity rules and submission state. Keep each field as a separate deliverable. A polished CV does not complete an unanswered selection question or submit a saved profile.

Separate three decisions:

- **Eligibility and screening:** an explicit mandatory credential, authorisation, experience threshold or question. Use the candidate's actual status. A missing requirement cannot be repaired with better prose. Distinguish employer criteria from law and clarify only material ambiguity.
- **Evidence visibility:** the person has relevant experience, but it is buried, described in unfamiliar terms or omitted from the submitted field.
- **Delivery:** the right copy must reach the right file and job-specific form. A general-profile edit does not prove an application changed.

Draft the useful work even when a material gap remains. State the gap briefly outside application copy and offer one realistic clarification or alternative. Do not label a person unsuitable solely because a desirable requirement is missing.

## 2. Place evidence where it will be assessed

For each essential requirement, locate the strongest supplied example and its period. Prefer direct evidence; use transferable evidence with its boundary. Put the example in the actual CV or requested answer, not only in a review table. If an older role supplies the strongest example, retain its date and make its relevance visible near the opening as space permits.

Use the vacancy's term when it accurately describes the work, followed by the concrete action. For example, keeping approved versions and withdrawing obsolete copies can support "document control"; merely filing documents may not. Allocating tasks or mentoring does not establish direct staff management. Recording an issue and forwarding it for action establishes coordination, not ownership of the remedy. A course or sandbox is learning, not production experience.

Preserve actual job titles. Use a target-role headline or a faithful functional explanation when useful. Do not turn sector vocabulary into experience with a new product, responsibility or regulated credential. No fixed keyword quota or density is appropriate.

Make every separate form answer self-contained within its limit. Reuse relevant facts accurately rather than telling the reviewer to see the CV. Answer the actual question first, then supply the example, action and supported result or deliverable. Do not invent an outcome to fill a formula. Remove unsupported old slogans from the final copy.

## 3. Calculate requested experience carefully

Count only periods doing the activity the field asks about. Career length, time at an employer, study and time doing a particular task are different quantities.

- Use supplied month/year boundaries. Merge overlapping qualifying periods before counting calendar duration; do not double-count concurrent work.
- Keep paid, freelance and volunteer work labelled. Follow the form's stated inclusion rules. Do not assume unpaid work counts for every employer.
- Use completed whole years if the form requires a whole number and does not specify another method. Explain the exact duration briefly when a note is available; do not round up into a qualifying threshold.
- Count direct-management responsibility only for its actual period, not an entire role that also included mentoring.
- Do not invent missing months or treat all part-time work as full-time. Ask one focused question when precision or the form's definition changes eligibility. Full-time equivalent duration needs actual hours and an applicable rule.

Use a small local calculation for overlapping or numerous intervals and inspect its inputs. A month-level calculation is not proof of exact days or recency of proficiency.

## 4. Check the deliverables, not the intention

Before returning the work, inspect what will actually be pasted or uploaded:

1. Every requested field/file exists and uses the correct format. Check individual word limits on the final text, allowing room for differences in portal counters.
2. Each supported essential requirement has visible evidence. If absent, restore the evidence or mark the real gap outside the draft. Do not add an unsupported claim to make the list complete.
3. Names, dates, role associations, figures, credentials and language levels agree across the CV and form answers. Anonymous fields omit prohibited identifiers even if the master CV contains them.
4. Application copy contains no editing instructions, internal gap notes or unfilled placeholders. Keep private review notes separate.
5. For files, render and inspect the actual final artifact and run local extraction. Use the existing preflight helper with a few critical source-grounded anchors, then read associations in the extracted text. Check size and page count against this vacancy, not a universal ATS rule.
6. For a portal problem, check the job-specific record and attachment. After a candidate-authorised submission, the final confirmation is a separate check. Never call a draft/profile a completed application or perform submission without authorisation.

Deliver the requested copy/files first, with only consequential remaining checks. Stop once the requested packet is usable within known facts and constraints. Never substitute a long optimisation report for the application.

## Basis and boundaries

These are editorial and process safeguards. Greenhouse's [Talent Matching documentation](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/41396009937307-Talent-Matching) describes employer-configured criteria and highlighted relevant text. Its separate [auto-reject rules](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360000653472-Auto-reject) can act on selected question answers. Neither supplies a universal score or a method to predict an employer decision.

A [published field experiment](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.04528) reports hiring gains from nongenerative writing assistance in an online labour market. That result motivates clear writing; it does not establish this skill's effects, a Gen X effect, or ATS acceptance uplift. Verified factual coverage, complete fields and sound local extraction are intermediate checks only.

# Artifact publication boundary

All candidates in this study are fictional. The named applicant, employment history and contact string in GX-64 were authored as a test fixture, then used by fresh agents to generate four CVs. They were not supplied as a real person's CV.

Automatic approval review rejected publication of a companion PDF, stating that its applicant-style content and metadata required specific permission for that document despite the fictional provenance. The maintainer checked the full text, metadata and generating execution record; the rejection remained for that PDF. No alternative format or channel was used to publish the rejected document.

Generated CV attachment files, rendered pages and extracted body copies are omitted from this public commit. This applies to all four document runs for a consistent archive boundary. Their exact hashes, sizes and the original task/response records remain public. The full generated artifacts were inspected locally by the maintainer and the blinded assessor before this publication restriction. Reported document observations are therefore agent review records with unavailable public attachment bytes, not a reader-reproducible file inspection.

Raw reviewer pair inputs are preserved as UTF-8 text files. Their historical paths identify the original assessment environment and are not public download links. The blind-review record lists the withheld input hashes separately. No real candidate data or live application was used.
